## 📖 What Is Scaler 2, Really?

At its surface, Scaler 2 is a music theory workstation that plugs into your DAW as a plugin. It detects the key of any MIDI or audio phrase you feed it, then suggests chord movements that actually belong together. Underneath, it's closer to a language engine. It doesn't just hand you a chord chart — it teaches you the grammar behind the chart.

You play three notes. Scaler identifies which scale they belong to, highlights compatible chords across seven octaves, offers voicing inversions, and lets you drag progressions straight into your timeline. The result: you compose with confidence instead of trial and error.

### 🎯 The Core Idea
Traditional music theory books assume you have years to study counterpoint before writing a single hook. Scaler flips that. It gives beginners immediate, usable results while giving experienced composers a shortcut they didn't know they wanted. Everyone wins.

## 🎧 Integration Flow — From Plugin to Project

### Step 1: Prepare Your Session
Open your DAW and create an empty instrument track. Insert the Scaler 2 plugin through your normal plugin browser.

### Step 2: Learn the Melody
Play or import a phrase. Scaler listens and identifies the scale, mode, and implied harmonic center.

### Step 3: Explore the Chord Grid
The interface reveals a matrix of compatible chords. Click any cell to preview it against your loop.

### Step 4: Shape the Voicing
Drag notes within the voicing panel to invert, spread, or stack the chord until it feels right.

### Step 5: Send It to Your Timeline
Drag the chord block onto an arrangement track, or export it as MIDI for further editing.

### Step 6: Perform Live
Switch to performance mode and trigger progressions on the fly using key bindings mapped to your controller.

Each step is optional. Some producers use Scaler as a full composition engine; others just want a quick chord suggestion. Both paths are supported.
